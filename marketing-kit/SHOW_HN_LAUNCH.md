# Estrategia y Copy para Show HN (Hacker News - DR 91)

El objetivo de publicar en Show HN no es vender directamente, sino presentar un recurso técnico de alto valor para la comunidad de desarrolladores y fundadores técnicos ("builders"), ganando cientos de visitas iniciales y enlaces entrantes *dofollow* de máxima autoridad.

---

## 📌 Datos de Publicación

- **Título sugerido:** `Show HN: AutoHub – A curated directory of n8n workflows and open-source SaaS alternatives`
- **Alternativa de título:** `Show HN: I built a free directory of production n8n workflows and open-source alternatives`
- **URL:** `https://autohub-directory.github.io`
- **Mejor momento:** Martes o miércoles a las 08:00 AM EST (13:00 UTC).

---

## 📝 Texto del Primer Comentario (Founder Comment)

```text
Hey HN,

Like many here running small businesses and agencies, I grew increasingly frustrated with SaaS pricing creep. Zapier charges steep per-task fees, Hootsuite costs over $99/mo for basic scheduling, and HubSpot quickly scales into hundreds of dollars as your contact list grows.

Platforms like n8n, Postiz, Chatwoot, and Twenty have proven that you can run production-grade operations on a $5/mo VPS without per-execution penalties. 

However, getting started with self-hosted automation often requires hours of boilerplate debugging: extracting webhooks, handling pagination on Google Places, or preventing OpenAI hallucination loops on WhatsApp.

So I built AutoHub (https://autohub-directory.github.io): a free, static, open directory of:
1. Tested, importable workflow JSON files (e.g. Google Maps lead enrichment with Firecrawl, WhatsApp AI assistants grounded in PDF catalogs).
2. Curated open-source alternatives to expensive SaaS with realistic self-hosting trade-offs.
3. Step-by-step pSEO integration guides with schema markup.

The entire site is static, built with Astro and Tailwind CSS, hosted for $0 on GitHub Pages with zero client-side tracking.

Would love feedback from other self-hosters and n8n operators: What repetitive workflows are still wasting your team's hours every week?
```
